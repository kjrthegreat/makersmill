About The Mill

More Than
a Venue.

The Makers Mill is downtown Somerset's creative heartbeat — a beautifully curated artisan market wrapped around a full bar and grill, with live music, games, and good people at every turn.

Whether you're hunting for a one-of-a-kind handmade piece, catching a live set, shooting pool, or just settling into a good drink in good company — you belong here.

Somerset, Kentucky

Built By This Town.
For This Town.

The Makers Mill exists because Somerset has always had creative people who needed a place to land. Musicians without a stage. Artists without a wall. Makers without a market.

Creativity and community thrive here as talent and skill are put on display — giving you the opportunity to make one-of-a-kind pieces a part of your life.
